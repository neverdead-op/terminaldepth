Production game seamless square base color texture for an ORIGINAL gothic Victorian house inspired by the architecture in input 1, NOT its realism. Input 2 defines our approved simplified hand-painted surface style.
Warm desaturated grey sandstone masonry in staggered horizontal courses, only 5 to 6 large rectangular ashlar stones across and 8 courses. Broad flat muted taupe, ivory-grey and olive-grey main color planes, one or two subdued angular painted patches on each stone, sparse tapered charcoal mortar lines, occasional bold chipped corner. Distinctly stylized painted 3D game material. NO photographic pores, NO tiny speckles, NO grain, NO dense cracks, NO realistic scanned rock, NO microdetail.
Flat even neutral diffuse illumination, no baked directional shadows, no vignette, no highlights or perspective. Seamlessly repeat both axes, edge to edge. Actual albedo swatch ONLY, no building, arches, windows, ornaments, leaves, text or border. Medium value to take cold ambient and warm lamp light in Godot.

Quiet broad shapes, not grainy detailed real wood. At least 85% smooth calm base color. Uneven thin dark joints. NO photographic grain, NO speckle, NO tiny scratches, NO busy grunge. Uniform flat neutral diffuse light with no shadows, directional shading, highlights, perspective or vignette. Repeat seamlessly left/right and top/bottom.
